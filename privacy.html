Privacy Policy

This page explains how MarketScope handles information while the project is in development.

Effective date: October 27, 2026 · Last updated: October 27, 2026

Current status: MarketScope is an early-stage project. The current public website is an informational landing page; it does not provide an Etsy account connection, and it does not currently operate an Etsy API integration.

1. Information we collect

The current public landing page does not ask you to create an account or submit personal information. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address and the contents of your request.

Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process technical information as part of delivering and securing the website. Please consult GitHub's own privacy information for details about its handling of website access data.

2. How we use email messages

We use messages sent to haiders021@gmail.com to reply to questions, provide project support, handle privacy requests, and maintain relevant correspondence. We do not intentionally sell your email address or message contents.

3. Etsy and connected accounts

MarketScope does not currently connect to Etsy accounts or retrieve Etsy account data through an API. Do not send us your Etsy password, access token, payment details, or other credentials by email.

If account connections or additional data features are introduced in a future version, this policy must be updated before those features are made available to users, and any required consent and authorization steps will be provided.

4. Storage, sharing, and retention

We retain support emails for as long as reasonably needed to respond to the request, maintain relevant records, and address follow-up issues. We do not currently operate a MarketScope user database for the public landing page. We may disclose information if required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the service, its users, or our rights.

5. Security

We take reasonable care with information sent to us, but email is not a secure channel for sensitive information. Please do not email passwords, authentication tokens, financial account details, or identity documents unless we provide a suitable secure process.

6. Your choices and requests

You can ask us about personal information contained in your support correspondence, or request its deletion where appropriate, by emailing haiders021@gmail.com. We may need to retain limited information where required by law or for legitimate recordkeeping.

7. Children

MarketScope is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this page as the project develops. The updated date at the top will indicate when changes were made. The policy in effect when a feature is released should describe that feature's actual data practices.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact haiders021@gmail.com.
